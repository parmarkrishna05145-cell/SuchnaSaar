What this tool does

A notice mixes three kinds of sentence: background that asks nothing of you, instructions you must act on, and deadlines. SuchnaSaar separates the three so the instruction is not buried in the legal drafting.

  • REFERENCE — background, preamble, boilerplate. No action needed.
  • ACTION — tells you to submit, apply, link, pay or produce.
  • DEADLINE — fixes a date or a period you must work to.
